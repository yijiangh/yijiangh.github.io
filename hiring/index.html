Prospective Students

I am recruiting multiple fully funded PhD students and Postdoctoral Researchers to join my new lab to advance robotic and design intelligence for automated construction, starting in January 2027 at the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the National University of Singapore.


Please read my research statement to learn more about my vision for the group.


If you think you are excited about the research directions and would like to join the group, please send me an email with the following materials with the subject line “PhD/Postdoc Application - Your Name”:

  • Your CV, including selected publications (if any)
  • A brief statement of research interests and career goals. Specially, please indicate why you think you are a good fit for the group and which research directions excite you the most.
  • Transcripts of your previous degrees (unofficial is fine)
  • Portfolio of previous projects (if any)
  • Contact information of 2-3 references
